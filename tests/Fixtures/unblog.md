# After the Storm

When walls fall,
the journey only truly begins.
Not with noise,
but with a quiet step
into tomorrow.

Where shadows once lived,
the light learns to return.
Not all at once,
but with every glance ahead
new hope grows.

The scars remain,
but they no longer speak of the end,
only of the fact
that we kept going.

By your side
even the darkness
no longer feels endless.
Tears turn into confidence,
ashes into a new beginning.

And if we find the courage
not just to mourn the world,
but to change it piece by piece,
then it's not just one sun
that rises over us,
but a thousand.
